A Mosasaurus could grow up to fifteen metres long. [pause]
Its jaws had a hinge in the middle, and a second row of teeth on the roof of its mouth. [pause]
It did not chew. It swallowed prey whole. [pause]
It was not a fish, and not a dinosaur. It was a giant sea lizard that breathed air. [long pause]
Sharks, dolphins, even whales. Nothing in today's ocean would be safe. [long pause]
But… [pause]
The very first Mosasaurus was found near Maastricht, in the Netherlands. [pause]
Its name means "lizard of the Meuse". [pause]
In 1795, French soldiers took the skull as war loot. It is still in Paris today. [long pause]
So remember. [pause]
The ocean was once ruled by a lizard longer than a bus. Would you still go swimming?
