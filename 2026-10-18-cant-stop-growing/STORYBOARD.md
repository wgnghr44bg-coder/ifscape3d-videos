## How the end numbers are calculated

- **grown** = `years * rate / 100` – At 25 cm (0.25 m) a year, eight years add 8 x 0.25 = 2 m of height.
- **h_end** = `h_start + grown` – 1.8 m today plus the 2 m you add in eight years gives 3.8 m - the first line of the counter.
- **weight_ratio** = `(h_end / h_start) ** 3` – A body 2.11 times taller, keeping its shape, holds 2.11 cubed = 9.4 times as much flesh, so the weight rises nine times.
- **bone_ratio** = `(h_end / h_start) ** 2` – The cross-section of a bone grows as the square of height, so at 2.11 times the height the bone is only 2.11 squared = 4.4 times stronger.
- **m_end** = `m_start * weight_ratio` – 75 kg times the ninefold rise in weight (9.4) gives about 705 kg - the second line of the counter.
- **loads** = `weight_ratio / bone_ratio` – Weight rises 9.4 times but the bone only 4.4 times, so every square centimetre of bone carries 9.4 / 4.4 = 2.1 times the load: about twice.

## Reality check

- step 7: Real doors are about two metres high, so a body past three metres cannot use an ordinary house at all: the open square becomes the only room in town that fits you, which is why you end up living and sleeping there.
- step 9: Everything in this scenario keeps its shape, so weight grows as the cube of height while bone cross-section grows as the square: every square centimetre of bone carries 2.1 times the load and every joint twice the pressure, the muscles must move nine times the mass with only four times the strength, and the heart must lift blood more than twice as high as it does now to reach the brain. Food rises more slowly than weight, because metabolism follows mass to the power 0.75: 9.4 times the mass is only about five times the food, roughly thirteen thousand kilocalories a day. Standing still is all that is left; walking ends first. Real giants come out thinner than this sum, because a real body does not keep its shape: the tallest man ever measured weighed 199 kg at 2.72 m, where the growing cube would give 258 kg.
- step 10: Nothing in the scenario ever closes the growth plates, so no signal turns the growth down: the body stays and keeps adding. The counter measures only the first eight years of that, which is why the closing line says the growth has not stopped.
